# Licensing, delivery specs and compliance (India focus)

Researched 16 Oct 2026 with sources. This is research, not legal advice: rules change, so re-check anything
marked M or L, and read the actual circular/terms before a regulated or paid job. Confidence: **H** official or
primary source, **M** reputable secondary or one primary source with gaps, **L** thin or conflicting.

## How to use this file
- Personal/family film kept private: section 1 (licences) mostly relaxes, sections 3 and 4 rarely apply.
- Anything posted publicly, for a client, a brand, or an ad: every section applies. Log each item in `sources.md`.
- Regulated verticals (pharma, BFSI): run the `compliance-reviewer` agent and get the client's legal/medical/compliance
  sign-off in writing before release.

**Vertical safe zone (one master for Reels/Shorts/TikTok):** keep captions, logos and CTAs inside x 108-972, y 288-1248
on 1080×1920 (Meta Reels: avoid top 14 %, bottom 35 %, 6 % sides). Verify in Meta's Ads Manager safe-zone tool. M

broadcast master; never ship one file for both.

## 3. Indian advertising and content compliance

### 3.1 ASCI (all advertising seen in India)
- Code: truthful/honest, non-offensive, against harmful products, fair competition. Social ads need an upfront label
  ("Ad", "Sponsored", "Collaboration"). [ASCI Code](https://www.ascionline.in/the-asci-code) H
- Disclaimers (reported Jan 2023): one per frame, max 2 lines, each held > 4 s, in the claim's language. M
- Influencer labels: ≤ 15 s video at least 3 s; 15 s to 2 min, one third of the length; ≥ 2 min, the whole brand
  section. Live: start and end. Virtual influencers say they are not human. Investment advice shows SEBI registration;
  health advice shows qualification. [guidelines PDF](https://www.ascionline.in/social/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ASCI-Influencer-Guidelines.pdf) H
- **AI / synthetic content in ads** (issued 29 Sep 2026, effective about **29 Dec 2026**): label when synthetic
  content materially influences a buying decision (synthetic ambassadors, a real person's cloned face/voice, demos of
  products that don't exist yet), e.g. "Audio/Video created using AI" / "enhanced using AI". Exempt: routine edits,
  decorative effects, subtitles, translations. **Using someone's likeness without consent is prohibited even with a
  label.** M-H
- CCPA 2022 misleading-ads guidelines: a disclaimer cannot cure a misleading claim; endorser due diligence; penalties up
  to ₹10 lakh, ₹50 lakh for repeats. M-H

### 3.2 Pharma
- **UCPMP 2024**: no promotion before approval and only within approved terms; balanced, verifiable claims; never an
  unqualified "safe" or "no side effects"; "new" only within 1 year; fair comparisons, no naming other brands without
  consent; **no HCP names or photos in promotion**; prescriber material carries holder name/address, generic name with
  brand, dosage, adverse reactions/warnings, "more information on request", date of last review; **AV material must be
  supported by printed material completing the Code**. [UCPMP 2024](https://www.pharma-dept.gov.in/sites/default/files/UCPMP%202024%20for%20website_0.pdf) H
- Prescription drugs (Schedule H/H1/X) can't be advertised to the public without Central Government sanction; a Sept
  2026 draft extends this to retailers/wholesalers; DCGI (12 Mar 2026) banned direct, indirect or **surrogate**
  promotion of prescription weight-loss drugs, incl. "disease awareness" and influencer campaigns that build brand
  recall. M-H
- **DMR Act 1954**: no drug ads for 54 scheduled conditions (diabetes, obesity, heart disease, BP, cancer, sterility,
  impotence ...); no misleading drug ads; exemption for material sent confidentially to registered medical
  practitioners. Drugs Rules Schedule J bars prevent/cure claims for listed conditions (fairness, hair growth, height).
- **HCP-facing video**: product content within the approved label, paired with full prescribing information; no
  doctors' names/faces; distribute only to RMPs (gated portal).
- **Patient-facing video**: no Rx brand, no drug name, no product cues; no cure claims for scheduled conditions;
  unbranded disease awareness ending "consult your doctor"; watch surrogate recall (colours, mascots, taglines).
- UK-targeted B2B (CDMO/CRO) films: ABPI/MHRA rules NOT researched; get the client's UK compliance review.

### 3.3 BFSI
- **Mutual funds**: "Mutual Fund investments are subject to market risks, read all scheme related documents
  carefully." Exact words. AV: on screen AND read in voice-over over at least 5 seconds. No indicative yields;
  performance-format rules apply. (SEBI MF Regulations 2026 may have moved the wording: verify.) H
- **SEBI Common Advertisement Code** (reported notified 8 Oct 2026): brokers, DPs, IAs, RAs, bond platforms, PMS, MFs.
  Celebrities only at brand/entity level with prior approval; other ads reported after release (24 h vs 3 working days:
  verify); ratings only from a PaRRVA; dark patterns banned. Read the circular before production. M
- **IA/RA**: "Investment in securities market are subject to market risks. Read all the related documents carefully
  before investing." audible in AV; show registration name and number; no assured returns, past performance, "best",
  "No. 1", or SEBI logo. M-H
- Finfluencers: regulated entities can't work with unregistered finfluencers (since 29 Aug 2024); education content may
  only use price data ≥ 3 months old (since 29 Jan 2025). M-H
- **IRDAI** (2024): board-approved ad policy; ULIPs/index-linked not advertised as "investment products", AV must say
  they differ from traditional insurance and carry risk; bonuses "not guaranteed"; no market-ranking claims. M-H
- **RBI**: digital lending KFS with APR; Feb 2026 draft on fair marketing (factual ads, disclosed rates/fees, explicit
  consent, no dark patterns): verify whether final. M

### 3.4 Synthetic media, privacy, music
- **IT Rules amendment** (in force 20 Feb 2026): synthetic content that "appears real" needs a prominent visible label,
  a prefixed audio disclosure for audio, and metadata where feasible; platforms make uploaders declare it. Answer the
  AI declaration honestly on every upload; burn in a visible label on realistic synthetic content. M-H
- **DPDP Act 2023**: Rules notified 13 Nov 2025; core consent duties from 13 May 2027 (a proposal would bring this to
  13 Nov 2026). Purely personal/domestic processing is exempt. Client films: written, purpose-specific consent from
  everyone filmed (capture, AI processing incl. face edits/voice clones, publication, retention, withdrawal); parental
  consent for children. M-H
- **Commercial songs**: sync needs licences from both the label (recording) and publisher (composition); PPL/IPRS
  public-performance licences don't cover sync. Private home playback is low risk; public posting gets Content ID
  claims (Shorts > 1 min with a claim are blocked). Brand films: licensed library, AI with clear terms, or original. M
- **Platform AI labels**: YouTube requires disclosure of realistic altered/synthetic content and auto-labels C2PA
  content; Meta requires disclosure of photorealistic AI video/audio. Cloning your own voice for a voice-over needs no
  YouTube disclosure. H/M

## 4. EU AI Act Article 50 and C2PA
- Art. 50 applies from **2 Aug 2026**: providers mark outputs machine-readably (systems on the market before that
  date have until 2 Dec 2026 under the Digital Omnibus); deployers disclose deepfakes (realistic people, places,
  events); for evidently artistic/fictional work a light, non-spoiling disclosure suffices; fines to €15M / 3 %. H text, M dates
- C2PA: re-encoding (ffmpeg, Remotion, WhatsApp, Facebook) strips manifests. To ship credentials, re-sign the final
  master (c2patool). Keep provider watermarks (SynthID) intact. M

## 5. Home projector / TV playback
- MP4 + faststart, H.264 High, 8-bit yuv420p, BT.709, constant 25/30 fps. 1080p30 Level 4.0/4.1 at 8-12 Mbps
  (cap ~20); 720p Level 3.1 at 5-8 Mbps. Avoid HEVC, 10-bit and 60 fps unless the manual lists them. H codec levels
- Bring a 720p backup. FAT32 has a 4 GB per-file limit: exFAT/NTFS for bigger masters.
- AAC-LC 48 kHz stereo 192-320 kbps. Built-in speakers are small, often effectively mono: check mono fold-down, keep
  dialogue forward, −16 to −14 LUFS, −1 to −2 dBTP. Bluetooth from projectors can lag picture: test lip-sync.
