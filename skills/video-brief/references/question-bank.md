# Question bank by project type

Ask only what is unknown; batch; mark a recommendation on every choice.

## Family / milestone (birthday, anniversary, "the first N years")
- Where will it play (device model, room size, speaker) and who is in the room (ages)?
- Should it be one continuous film or episodes/chapters? (Episodes that grow each year work well for birthdays.)
- Who narrates: a character voice, a parent (recorded or cloned with consent), or captions only?
- Everyone who must appear, how the child addresses each, correct spellings; anyone deceased and the honorific.
- Years or events to leave out; any period that needs gentle handling and who decides how it is shown.
- The family's hero objects and places (vehicle, house, a toy, a pet); proof photos.
- Rituals and festivals in the material: what the family calls them (or whether to name them at all).
- Songs, lullabies, hums the family actually sings (record them: real family voices beat any library).
- Letters or wishes from the parents to include (set exactly as written; never edit their words).
- Where else material lives: the other parent's phone, grandparents' phones, pen drives, old accounts.
- Should the film interact with the room (pauses for children to answer, a sing-along, a live moment)?

## Memorial / tribute
- Who is it for (the family, a gathering, posterity)? Tone: celebration of a life, or grief-holding?
- Their own voice: recordings, videos, voice notes that exist (use real voice; never clone the deceased).
- Faith or cultural customs to respect in text, music and imagery; honorifics for the deceased.
- People who must be included; people whose inclusion needs a family decision.
- Music they loved (rights: private screening vs public posting).

## Wedding / couple
- Story frame (how they met, the families, the day itself); events of the wedding and their names.
- Any family sensitivities; former partners or estranged relatives excluded or not.
- Guest messages to collect (format, deadline, an inbox folder).

## Brand film / company story
- The one sentence the viewer should remember; the audience (customers, recruits, investors, internal).
- Brand kit: logo files, colours, fonts (licensed for video?), voice and tone guide, do's and don'ts.
- People on screen: employees/customers with written consent (capture, AI processing, publication, withdrawal).
- Claims that will be made, with substantiation; competitors that must not be named.
- Distribution: platforms, paid or organic, aspect ratios, captions/subtitles languages, AI disclosure needs.
- Approvals chain and legal/compliance reviewer; deadline for each round.

## Explainer / product video
- The audience's current understanding and the single change in understanding we want.
- The product facts: what it does, for whom, the proof, the limits; approved terminology.
- Screens, demos, data to show; who provides them; any unreleased features (ASCI: label demos of products that
  do not exist yet if synthetic).
- Length per platform; voice and captions languages.

## Ad (TV, OTT, social)
- Objective, offer, CTA, mandatory supers/disclaimers and how long they must hold (see compliance doc).
- Formats and lengths (6 s, 15 s, 30 s; 16:9, 1:1, 9:16) and safe zones per platform.
- Talent, voice and music rights (sync + master licences, or licensed library / original / AI with clear terms).
- Brand safety and category rules (ASCI; category-specific codes).

## Pharma (route to marketing-video + compliance-reviewer)
- HCP-facing or patient/public-facing? Distribution channel (gated HCP portal vs public)?
- Product approval status and the approved label (claims must stay within it); PI to accompany AV material.
- Any condition in the DMR Act schedule or Schedule J (no cure/prevent claims to the public).
- Doctors on screen? (UCPMP 2024: no HCP names or photos in promotion.)
- Medical/legal/regulatory reviewer and sign-off process; date of last review shown on the material.
- Target markets beyond India (UK/EU/US rules differ: get local review).

## BFSI (route to marketing-video + compliance-reviewer)
- Entity type (MF/AMC, IA/RA, broker, insurer, bank/NBFC, lender) and the regulator's ad code that applies.
- Mandatory warnings (exact words, on screen and voice-over, minimum duration), registration numbers to show.
- Performance or return figures (format rules; no assured returns), ratings, celebrity use (prior approval).
- Compliance officer sign-off and post-release reporting obligations.
