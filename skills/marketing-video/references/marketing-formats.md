# Marketing formats, hooks and sign-off

## Hook patterns (first 3 seconds, readable muted)

| Pattern | Example shape |
|---|---|
| The cost of the problem | a number on screen + the moment it hurts ("3 hours a week on reconciliation") |
| The surprising fact | one sourced fact, big type, the source in small type |
| The outcome first | the after-state, then "here's how" |
| The customer's words | a real customer's line to camera (consent on file), subtitled |
| The question | a question the audience is already asking, answered by the video |
| The demo | the product doing the thing in the first second |

## Explainer scene grammar

One scene = one idea = one picture = one line. Abstract words get a visual metaphor or a real screen. Numbers
appear on screen exactly as said. The last scene repeats the one message and shows the CTA.

## Testimonials and case studies

Real customers only, with signed consent; their words unedited in meaning (trims allowed, no recombining
sentences into a new claim); their claims go in the claims ledger like any other; subtitles verbatim.
