# Sponsors

These plugins are MIT-licensed, free to use commercially, and ship without telemetry.
What sponsorship pays for is the unglamorous half: provider API updates when a voice or
AI service ships a breaking version, platform spec refreshes, compliance passes when
guidance moves, and issue triage.

If they save your team time, you can [sponsor the work](https://github.com/sponsors/indranilbanerjee).
